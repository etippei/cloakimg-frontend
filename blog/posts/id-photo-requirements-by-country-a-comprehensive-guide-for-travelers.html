ID Photo Requirements by Country: A Comprehensive Guide for Travelers (2026)

Applying for a passport, visa, or national ID card is stressful enough without worrying about photo rejection. Yet photo requirements vary significantly by country, and even a minor deviation can lead to application delays or rejection. This comprehensive guide covers ID photo requirements for major countries worldwide, helping you get it right the first time.

Why ID Photos Get Rejected

Before diving into specific requirements, it's helpful to understand why ID photos are commonly rejected:

Rejection Reason How to Avoid It
Wrong background color Use the correct background color per country
Head size incorrect Follow exact measurement guidelines
Facial expression wrong Keep neutral expression, mouth closed
Shadows on face or background Ensure even, uniform lighting
Hair covering eyes or face Keep hair off your face
Glasses with glare Remove glasses or use non-reflective lenses
Printed on wrong paper Use professional photo paper
Older than 6 months Take fresh photos for each application

United States Passport Photos

Requirements

  • Dimensions: 2 x 2 inches (51 x 51 mm)
  • Background: White or off-white, no shadows
  • Head Size: 1 to 1 3/8 inches (25-35 mm) from chin to top of hair
  • Expression: Neutral, mouth closed, eyes open
  • Head Position: Facing forward, centered
  • Recent: Must be taken within last 6 months

Additional Rules

  • No uniforms or camouflage clothing
  • No hats or head coverings (except religious)
  • No headphones, glasses with tinted lenses, or heavy frames
  • No selfies or mirror photos
  • Photo must be printed on photo-quality paper

United Kingdom Passport Photos

Requirements

  • Dimensions: 35 x 45 mm (standard UK)
  • Background: Cream or light grey (not white)
  • Head Size: 29-34 mm from chin to crown
  • Expression: Neutral, no smiling
  • Head Position: Facing forward, centered

Additional Rules

  • No glasses with tinted lenses
  • No heavy head coverings (except religious)
  • No strong shadows on face or background
  • No red-eye
  • Photos must be taken within the last month

Schengen Area (EU) Passport Photos

Requirements

  • Dimensions: 35 x 45 mm
  • Background: Light, uniform (white or light grey)
  • Head Size: 70-80% of photo height
  • Expression: Neutral, mouth closed
  • Head Position: Facing forward

Countries Using Schengen Requirements

Austria, Belgium, France, Germany, Greece, Italy, Netherlands, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, and others.

Additional Rules

  • No shadows on face
  • No hair covering eyes
  • No glasses with thick frames
  • No selfies

Canada Passport Photos

Requirements

  • Dimensions: 50 x 70 mm (2 x 2 3/4 inches)
  • Background: White or light-colored
  • Head Size: 31-36 mm from chin to crown
  • Expression: Neutral, mouth closed
  • Head Position: Facing forward

Additional Rules

  • Photos must be taken by a commercial photographer
  • Must be printed on photo-quality paper
  • No smiles, frowns, or raised eyebrows
  • No red-eye
  • Must have date stamp on back

Australia Passport Photos

Requirements

  • Dimensions: 35 x 45 mm
  • Background: White or light grey
  • Head Size: 32-36 mm from chin to crown
  • Expression: Neutral, mouth closed
  • Head Position: Facing forward

Additional Rules

  • No shadows on face or background
  • No "red-eye" effect
  • No uniform or camouflage
  • No hats, scarves, or head coverings (except religious)
  • Photos must be within last 6 months

China Passport Photos

Requirements

  • Dimensions: 33 x 48 mm
  • Background: White
  • Head Size: 21-24 mm width, 28-33 mm height
  • Expression: Neutral, mouth closed
  • Head Position: Facing forward

Additional Rules

  • Both ears must be visible
  • Light clothing preferred (avoid white)
  • No strong contrast lighting
  • Must be taken within the last 6 months

Japan Passport Photos

Requirements

  • Dimensions: 35 x 45 mm
  • Background: White
  • Head Size: 26-30 mm from chin to crown
  • Expression: Neutral, mouth closed
  • Head Position: Facing forward

Additional Rules

  • No hats, glasses, or other accessories
  • No visible jewelry near face
  • Must be taken within the last 6 months

India Passport Photos

Requirements

  • Dimensions: 51 x 51 mm (2 x 2 inches)
  • Background: White
  • Head Size: 25-35 mm from chin to crown
  • Expression: Neutral, mouth closed
  • Head Position: Facing forward

Additional Rules

  • No glasses except for medical reasons
  • No uniforms
  • No selfies
  • Photos must be within last 6 months

Brazil Passport Photos

Requirements

  • Dimensions: 35 x 45 mm
  • Background: White
  • Head Size: 29-32 mm from chin to crown
  • Expression: Neutral, mouth closed
  • Head Position: Facing forward

Additional Rules

  • No glasses
  • No accessories on head
  • No uniforms
  • Must be taken within the last 6 months
