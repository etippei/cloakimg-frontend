How to Create Your AI LinkedIn Headshot

Step 1: Take a Good Source Photo

Even AI can't fix a bad source image. Start with a quality photo:

Lighting: - Face a window or use soft, even lighting - Avoid harsh shadows on your face - Ensure your face is well-lit, not backlit

Background: - Stand against a plain wall or use a simple background - Don't worry too much about the background—AI can replace it

Composition: - Face the camera directly - Show your head and shoulders - Keep a neutral, pleasant expression - Ensure your face is in focus

Attire: - Wear professional clothing - Solid colors work best - Avoid busy patterns

Step 2: Remove and Replace the Background

The background of your photo can make or break your professional look. AI background removal makes it easy to create a clean, professional backdrop.

How to do it: 1. Upload your photo to an AI background remover like CloakImg AI 2. The AI automatically removes the background 3. Choose a professional background color (white, light gray, or subtle gradient) 4. Download your photo with the new background

Best background colors for LinkedIn: - White — Clean, classic, professional - Light gray — Soft, modern, versatile - Navy blue — Trustworthy, corporate - Neutral tones — Warm, approachable

Step 3: Crop for LinkedIn

LinkedIn profile photos display as circles, so proper cropping is essential.

Cropping guidelines: - Your face should fill about 60% of the frame - Center your face in the square - Include the top of your shoulders - Leave a small margin around your head

AI resizing tools can help you crop to the exact dimensions needed (400 x 400 pixels or larger).

Step 4: Enhance Lighting and Quality

AI tools can improve the quality of your photo:

  • Brightness adjustment — Ensure your face is well-lit
  • Contrast enhancement — Make features stand out
  • Color correction — Fix any color casts
  • Sharpening — Ensure your features are crisp

CloakImg AI's upscaler can also improve resolution if your source photo is low quality.
