How to Convert HEIC to JPG: A Complete Guide for iPhone Users (2026)

If you've ever tried to upload an iPhone photo to a website, send it via email, or open it on a Windows computer, you may have encountered the dreaded HEIC format. While HEIC offers excellent quality at smaller file sizes, it's not universally supported. This guide explains what HEIC is, why it causes problems, and how to convert HEIC to JPG quickly and easily.

What Is HEIC and Why Does Apple Use It?

HEIC stands for High Efficiency Image Container. It's a modern image format based on the HEVC (High Efficiency Video Coding) standard. Apple adopted HEIC as the default photo format starting with iOS 11 in 2017.

Why Apple Chose HEIC

Benefit Explanation
Smaller file sizes HEIC files are about 50% smaller than equivalent JPG files
Better quality More detail preserved at the same file size
Advanced features Supports multiple images, depth data, and editing history
Efficient storage More photos fit on your device

The Problem with HEIC

Despite its technical advantages, HEIC has a major drawback: limited compatibility. Many platforms, devices, and software programs simply don't support the format.

Common HEIC Problems

1. Windows Compatibility Issues

Windows 10 and 11 don't natively support HEIC without additional codecs. This means you can't open iPhone photos on a Windows PC without installing extra software.

2. Website Upload Problems

Many content management systems, e-commerce platforms, and social media schedulers reject HEIC files. You'll often see an error message like "Unsupported file format."

3. Email Attachment Limitations

Some email clients and providers don't support HEIC attachments. Recipients may not be able to view your photos.

4. Older Software Limitations

Older versions of Photoshop, Lightroom, and other editing software may not support HEIC.

5. Browser Incompatibility

Not all browsers render HEIC images. If you upload a HEIC file to a website, visitors using unsupported browsers will see a broken image icon.

When You Should Convert HEIC to JPG

1. Uploading to Websites

If you're uploading product photos to an e-commerce platform, adding images to a blog, or sharing on social media, JPG is the safer choice.

2. Sending via Email

For maximum compatibility, convert HEIC to JPG before attaching photos to emails.

3. Using Windows PCs

If you work with Windows computers, converting to JPG ensures your photos open without additional software.

4. Sharing with Non-Apple Users

Android users and Windows users may not be able to open HEIC files. Converting to JPG ensures everyone can view your photos.

5. Editing in Older Software

If you use older editing software that doesn't support HEIC, convert to JPG first.

6. Printing Services

Many online and retail printing services prefer JPG files. Converting ensures your prints come out correctly.

How to Convert HEIC to JPG

Method 1: Change iPhone Settings (Prevent Future HEIC Files)

If you want to stop your iPhone from creating HEIC files in the first place:

  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap Camera
  3. Tap Formats
  4. Select Most Compatible

This will make your iPhone capture photos in JPG format instead of HEIC. Note that this increases file sizes.

Method 2: Convert Individual Photos on iPhone

For existing HEIC photos on your iPhone:

  1. Open the Photos app
  2. Select the photo you want to convert
  3. Tap the Share button
  4. Choose Copy Photo (this copies it as JPG in some versions)
  5. Or use Save to Files and select JPG format
